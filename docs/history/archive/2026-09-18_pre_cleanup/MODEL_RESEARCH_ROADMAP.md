## 1. Starting point: what is established

The corrected quarterly benchmark is complete; see
[ASOF_ADDITIVE_REFERENCE_RESULTS.md](ASOF_ADDITIVE_REFERENCE_RESULTS.md).
Across 5,607 primary lineup-quarter cells / 726,364 possessions, additive versus
designated structural MAE is 8.0920 / 8.1461, pooled raw r² .12043 / .10719.
The season-paired MAE gap is small and inconclusive; additive's ranking and
squared-error advantages are more consistent. Novel-lineup MAE is nearly tied.
These are raw offensive outcomes, not opponent-adjusted targets.

Current structural ingredients: dated EB player profiles, finite usage budget,
prior-only opportunity-share correction, conditional channel valuation,
shooting surplus, and a sign-constrained passing-deficit penalty. No known-pair
chemistry. The designated arm uses 180-day role and 730-day shooting half-lives;
recent league valuation and opportunity calibration use 365-day half-lives.
Final point calibration is expanding, not recency-weighted.

Important absences from that scorer:

- A learned effect of a player's presence on **other players'** shot locations,
  two/three/FT mix, or conversion conditional on location.
- A rich response of individual role/efficiency to a new teammate context.
- A physical miss/rebound/extra-attempt loop or teammates' rebound facilitation.
- Actual-opponent inputs, full defensive assignments, and matchup predictions.
- Completed arbitrary-lineup/OOD mean uncertainty. Older finite-batch coverage
  results do not establish this for the current quarterly model.

Composition can change team totals without changing a teammate's behavior.
Do not describe that arithmetic as a measured teammate response. Historical
shooter profiles can contain benefits supplied by old teammates; transporting
the profile alone can misattribute those benefits to the shooter.

**Research-history clarification:** these questions have had preliminary tests,
even though those responses are not integrated into the current score.
[CREATION_EXPERIMENT.md](CREATION_EXPERIMENT.md) found workload-to-shot-mix
signal and passing-to-assisted-made-share / turnover signal. Additional
passing-to-shot-mix signal was inconclusive; shooting improvements were not
established. This was an annual player-game experiment with 36.67% retained
player-possession exposure, not a current-quarter point-model result. Reuse
that evidence and implementation; do not relabel the old experiment as unrun.

### R01. Strength of defenses faced

Question: how much of the apparent error in a lineup's future offensive rate
comes from the actual defenses it encountered?

First candidate is a common prior-only defensive reference using the actual
opposing five and exposure weights, with a declared sign/centering convention.
Either add the same opponent term to both forecasts or subtract it from the
common target; do not silently combine both operations. Preserve the raw
comparison and print adjusted results separately. Saved additive defensive
coefficients are a convenient sensitivity, not independent defensive truth.
If conclusions depend on this choice, use a separately specified defense
estimator as a second sensitivity rather than declaring the issue solved.

Deliverable: adjusted aggregate metrics and updated high-exposure case table,
showing each lineup's opponent adjustment. No contemporaneous full-season
defensive ratings or future opponent outcomes in the reference fit. Actual
future opponent composition is explanatory conditioning for this diagnostic,
not information available to a generic pre-season forecast.

### R02. Quantity versus conversion/valuation failure

Use [the corrected disagreement review](reports/asof_centered_disagreement_review.md).
Denver's Westbrook/Jokić/Murray/Porter/Braun case has close 3PA/FTA totals but
too many predicted turnovers and substantial remaining point error. Other
persistent cases have different errors; no universal explanation is established.

Decompose prediction/observation gaps into shot/FT/TO volumes, location mix,
shooting, rebounding, and the conditional valuation/calibration layer. An
observed-count or observed-shooting substitution may help locate the problem,
but is an **oracle diagnostic**, never a forward model improvement. Some
substitutions violate the fixed budget: disclose that rather than pretending
they are a valid candidate offense. Report order dependence/interactions when
several quantities change. Keep quarter-specific fits before averaging.

Deliverable: a basketball-readable decomposition for contrasting failure cases
plus the full qualifying population. The selected worst cases are diagnostic,
not an independent confirmation set; retain early/late persistence and noise
checks without treating repeated quarters as independent seasons.

### R03. How teammates change shot mix

**Opened 2026-09-07:** `TEAMMATE_INFLUENCE_EXPERIMENT.md` gives this direction
a single estimand, an estimator and pre-registered acceptance tests, plus the
reconnaissance and screen that motivate it.

Target the **remaining players'** attempts, not the incoming player's own shots:
2PA/3PA/FTA/TO shares plus the conditional location distribution within FGA.
Candidate mechanisms: teammate creation, drive-and-kick, off-ball spacing,
screening/rolling, and changes in primary versus secondary workload.

First comparison should be nested: current calibrated opportunity model;
plus supported workload response; plus one explicitly named teammate response.
Center context changes on the context in which the prior profile was earned.
Use properly normalized probabilities and preserve the event budget. Generic
era/category correction stays as a control so it cannot be relabeled synergy.

Existing evidence: workload improves shot-mix cross-entropy in 10/11 annual
seasons; extra passing beyond workload is inconclusive. A new test must add a
meaningful change, such as strict-date states, opponent conditioning, improved
creation/spacing inputs, or integrated point transport—not just rerun the old
assist-rate feature. Confirm intermediate outcomes and final points on the
same population; independently inspect C and U before any promotion decision.

### R04. How teammates change conversion at a given location

**Opened 2026-09-07:** `TEAMMATE_INFLUENCE_EXPERIMENT.md` gives this direction
a single estimand, an estimator and pre-registered acceptance tests, plus the
reconnaissance and screen that motivate it.

Separate three estimands: changes in shot location; changes in difficulty at
that location; conversion above difficulty expectation. Location-adjusted
make/miss residuals in current data combine unobserved shot quality, shooter
state, opposing defense and noise. Call them conditional conversion outcomes,
not direct measurements of openness.

Potential design: prior shooter/location/era reference, other-four-player
context, and opponent/deployment controls, evaluated on future attempts with
appropriate shrinkage. Assess variation within shooters and different teammate
contexts; absence/trade comparisons are informative but not randomized.
Player/team-game controls can remove a shared team-wide creator effect, so
specify what variation each estimator identifies. Avoid adjusting away the
shot-quality pathway when estimating its total effect.

The data reconnaissance identifies contested-shot splits and newer vendor
expected-FG%/Gravity as possible inputs or diagnostic targets. Aggregate
shooter stats do not automatically attach a creator to each attempt. Vendor
predictions are not ground truth and may lack historically safe model versions.
Use negative controls such as FT% thoughtfully; a null placebo does not prove
causality. Evaluate probabilities and future points, not regression significance
alone. No automatic bonus for more assisted makes or higher potential assists.

### R05. Role flexibility, initiation, and finishing

Question: which players can change jobs without losing their effectiveness?
Westbrook adapting to a secondary role is different from updating his recent
average usage. Estimate aptitude versus assigned role with partial pooling;
consider touches, time on ball, pull-ups, catch-and-shoot, screening and passing
opportunities as role descriptors. Distinguish common lineup load shifts from
relative player shifts. The alpha-1 allocator won direct role tests; alpha 3
was a ranking diagnostic, not an accurate literal assignment rule.

Creation shortages should eventually change attempted actions, turnovers or
shot difficulty, not merely append a total-rating penalty. Off-ball finishing
efficiency cannot be assumed to survive forced self-creation. A full latent
role hierarchy is an option, not the next automatic large build. Read
`ROLE_MODEL.md` and `CREATION_EXPERIMENT.md` before designing a bounded extension.

### R06. Value an offense without misleading event coefficients

Consider explicit shot probabilities and a consistent possession/event flow
instead of relying entirely on conditional point-regression coefficients.
The negative ORB coefficient in the current regression is not causal rebound
value, and a fixed budget does not grant the extra attempts boards create.
Separate creation, conversion, misses, recovery and renewed opportunities;
define technical/free-throw bookkeeping and avoid paying twice for an assist,
rebound or already-valued make. Test a bounded replacement of one layer before
attempting a complete simulator. Any improved coherence can justify a modest
predictive tradeoff, but measure the tradeoff and re-estimate uncertainty.

### R07. Rebounding: getting a board versus making the team get it

Preserve `REBOUND_EXPERIMENT.md`'s completed three runs. Miss-conditioned
capture with competition improved rebound forecasts and isolated coherence,
but the latest-state point MAE worsened (7.528 versus 7.474 in that older rig).
These are not the current quarterly benchmark numbers.

Next candidates: teammate facilitation/box-outs, contested opportunity capture,
shot-location-dependent rebound supply, opponent rebounding, and the cost of
crashing versus transition defense. Test team ORB separately from individual
credited ORB. Adam's examples: Adams improving team boards, Robinson more often
collecting them himself, Hart exceptional for his size. These are qualitative
checks, not required rankings or player-specific coefficients. No arbitrary
ORB cap or resurrected negative rebound penalty to repair five-center offense.

### R08. Defense and genuine matchups

Start with R01, then distinguish perimeter containment, help/rim protection,
fouling, turnovers forced and defensive rebounds. Eventually assign coverage
to the opposing offense's roles, allowing help and switches. Additional rim
protectors may have diminishing returns while an exposed perimeter assignment
can dominate; these are hypotheses/structural choices to name and test.

The old unrestricted identity-level off×def block had very low reliability.
That is a reason not to repeat it, not a proof that richer profile-based
defensive mechanisms can never work. Team scheme and deployment need separate
handling. Deliver offensive rates for both teams and margin, with permutation
invariance and team-swap antisymmetry; an additive defensive placeholder must

### R16. Lineup-adjusted capability profiles for both sides

**Status: planned, 2026-09-18.** Originally parked while v15 ran; its completed
review now informs `JOINT_CAPABILITY_V16_PLAN.md`, requested by Adam. That plan
specifies the temporal feature library, likelihoods, anchoring, contrasts and
uncertainty limitations. Planning does not mean fits have started: implementation,
operational acceptance and production launch are separate next steps.

**Hypothesis:** regularized lineup models may estimate more transferable
individual capabilities than raw on-floor averages. Use those multidimensional
estimates as inputs to a contextual NN, rather than collapsing each player to
one point rating or requiring the final prediction to add player coefficients.
Ridge estimates average contributions in observed contexts; the NN can learn
how those capabilities combine. This is a hypothesis, not an established gain.

Potential targets:

- **Defense:** opponent shot distribution, conversion within location,
  turnovers forced, free throws conceded, and recovery conditional on a
  rebound opportunity. Include both offensive and defensive identities in
  estimation, with a dated league baseline, so teammates and opponent skill
  are adjusted for jointly.
- **Offense, own capabilities:** context-adjusted shooting, turnover avoidance,
  foul drawing and rebounding. Define the outcome and opportunity denominator
  explicitly; an adjusted team rate is not automatically a personal skill.
- **Offense, teammate influence:** changes in teammates' shot mix, making,
  turnovers and offensive rebound recovery. Separate the focal player's own
  production from teammate-only outcomes, or explicitly model shooter identity,
  before calling a coefficient facilitation. A great scorer raising team FG%
  does not establish that he improved his teammates' shooting.

For shot mix, consider an L2-regularized multinomial model so probabilities
remain positive and sum to one. Use appropriate attempt/opportunity exposure
for conversion and rebounding; regularized linear fits are a useful simple
control, not permission to export invalid probabilities. Coefficients are
signed conditional associations, not fractions of causal credit. Preserve
raw own-action/style information; start by testing adjusted features alongside
raw profiles rather than assuming replacement is better. No known-pair terms
or player-ID embeddings in the NN are implied by estimating per-player inputs.

**What prior work does and does not settle:**

- V5_OPP adjusted offensive states for a scalar historical opponent-strength
  summary; see `joint_capability_v5_data.py` / `joint_capability_v5_fit.py` and
  `reports/joint_capability_v5_review.md`. Its null point result does not test
  this multidimensional teammate-and-opponent-adjusted representation.
- `experiments/onfloor_influence.py` already fits channel-specific ridge effects
  with all ten identities. `TEAMMATE_INFLUENCE_EXPERIMENT.md` section 9 records
  defensive-channel evidence. Those are corrections relative to an older
  structural offset, not already validated NN capability inputs. Audit their
  estimand, dates, centering and reuse eligibility instead of silently treating
  residual coefficients as standalone skill or repeating completed work.

**Candidate comparisons, to preregister later:** keep architecture, populations,
training budget and calibration policy matched; compare raw profiles with
offense-only augmentation, defense-only augmentation and both. Include an
adjusted-only representation ablation and a simple non-neural adjusted-channel
readout to distinguish better attribution from better nonlinear combination.
Changing inputs requires NN refits, not swapping profiles into a frozen NN.
Do not separately add the same estimated effect twice in the accounting.

**Required safeguards and evaluation:**

- Training-example profiles must themselves be strictly prior or generated by
  temporal out-of-fold fits that exclude their own outcomes. Fitting profiles
  on the full NN training window and feeding them back to those same rows is
  not an honest online representation. Inner validation, outer evaluation,
  penalty selection, normalization and calibration each respect their cutoffs.
- Include dated environment terms and explicit, prior-only centering to prevent
  offensive/defensive level nonidentifiability from reintroducing the earlier
  additive gauge problem. Do not recenter using an evaluation-quarter mean.
- Lineup variation identifies attribution; shrinkage stabilizes it, but cannot
  separate players who always share the floor or remove unobserved coaching,
  scheme and deployment. Carry uncertainty/identification support, not merely
  minutes played; correlated coefficient uncertainty matters for lineup sums
  and swaps. Cold players need disclosed pooled priors.
- Judge forward channel and point accuracy, novel/recurring and existing
  trade/role-change cohorts, counterfactual mechanisms and OOD uncertainty on
  equal footing. Retain additive and matched opponent-aware NN/blend controls.
  Do not force named-player rankings, infer causal portability from ridge, or
  mistake coefficient/seed uncertainty for validated latent-lineup coverage.
