### V19 review and v20 framing — agreed 2026-10-02

Adam supports the three design reflections below and is particularly interested
in **game-situation adjustment when estimating player profiles**, not only in
the downstream NN decoder. These are planning priorities, not a frozen v20
experiment or authorization to modify the running v19 campaign.

1. **Identify capabilities before elaborating mechanisms.** Current raw and
   lineup-adjusted profiles still carry historical deployment context. Consider
   fitting shared situation effects alongside player effects, with every profile,
   normalizer and tuning step respecting its own training/inner/final cutoff.
   Compare profile adjustment separately from decoder adjustment; a matched
   profile-by-decoder factorial is a candidate, not a settled arm roster. New
   profiles may require NN refits; do not assume frozen weights transfer to a
   changed input meaning. Clock and margin are not interchangeable: margin can
   be a consequence of player quality. Adjustment is not automatically causal
   ability identification. Use controlled synthetic recoverability checks and
   supported real role-change/transport evidence, beyond arithmetic/gradient
   tests. Assist-derived supply remains a proxy, not identified creation capacity.
2. **Treat training stabilization as a research question.** V19's interim
   checkpoint averaging materially improves several joint arms, but neither
   proves a stopping-rule bug nor establishes the final winner. In the completed
   review separate native versus calibrated gains, quarter bias versus ranking,
   seed/epoch sensitivity, and conditional versus reference validation behavior.
   Consider a small predeclared, equally applied stabilization comparison before
   another architecture sweep; no outer-outcome epoch selection or mid-run tuning.
3. **Give all three standards modeling effort, not just reporting.** Preserve
   the original predictive benchmark while distinguishing realized-deployment
   prediction from common-environment product ratings. A conditional-context gain
   alone does not show better player values. Give prospective transport and
   latent-quality uncertainty concrete hypotheses and acceptance evidence; finite
   game intervals, seed spread and a coverage gate do not solve arbitrary-lineup
   uncertainty. Keep basketball checks falsifiable, not forced rankings.

Sequence: finish and review v19 under its frozen protocol, synthesize its point,
event, coherence and uncertainty findings against these questions, then agree a
bounded v20 design. Do not interpret interest in upstream adjustment as permission
