Lineup Lab

Pick any five players, each as of one season between 2001–02 and 2025–26. The model adds up what each player brings, then applies combination effects: pairs of players who play differently together than apart. You get offense, defense and net (points scored minus points allowed), all per 100 possessions, with an 80% range for the lineup's long-run level (an 8-in-10 span). How the model works.

    The forecast is a neural model of every possession's events, calibrated on earlier seasons, plus learned and measured combination effects. It uses no player identity, only each player-season's measured habits, skills and height; a player-season (for example Curry 2025–26) is the player as measured through the end of that season. "League average" means the 2025–26 lineups that played at least 100 possessions. Overall quality and the breakdowns are approximations of the model, and ranges for lineups nobody has played rest on named assumptions. How the model works, including the data, the player estimates, the range and the accuracy checks.
