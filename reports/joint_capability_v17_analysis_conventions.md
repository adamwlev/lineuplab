# v17 derived-output conventions

Implementation details fixed before production outcomes; supplements the frozen
v17 plan without changing its arms, training, populations or interpretation.

- Fixture evaluation is direct plus its fixed half blend. At each of the ten
  frozen dates, evaluate every ordered pair in the union of registered fixtures
  and the three frozen prior-common defensive units, including reverse matches.
  No exhaustive average-opponent integration for synthetic fixtures. All seven
  arms and three seeds are included. Export native per-player/location ledgers,
  creation allocation and calibration separately. Mixed-era raw Korver input
  does not transport his adjusted, rebound or defensive states.
- For the three flow arms, capacity curves multiply the **capacity entering the
  allocator only** by 0,.5,1,2. Keep all player profiles, neural messages, demand,
  load and FT-shortage response fixed. These are saved-weight mechanism
  interventions, not new forecasts or causal assist effects. No monotonic total
  rating or player-rank acceptance condition. Independently permute offensive
  and defensive slots; no-self means slot identity, not player-ID identity in
  intentionally duplicated profiles. Report allocation slack explicitly.
- Retain all analogue members and their missing-height/low-support flags. Reuse
  the verified v16 dated descriptors by exact origin/off5 keys; never reuse old
  forecasts in place of v17 outputs. Top/bottom10 use actual rating, >=100
  possessions, separately for each registered analogue cohort, with ties broken
  by origin and sorted player IDs. This is outcome-selected description only.
- Registered inference uses only the16 half-view/all/broad contrasts. For a
  partial engineering pilot print descriptive deltas but no confirmatory p-values
  or Holm claims. Production inference requires all44 primary quarters and
  three-seed prediction means; seeds are not independent observations.
- U0 summaries show both all-off5 and broad>=50 populations, equal-cell and
  possession-weighted coverage, width and interval score, and unidentified
  counts. U1 summarizes the identical balanced-date pairs, equal-pair proper
  scores and finite-half coverage, plus unidentified counts. All-model matched
  identified counts are explicit. Candidate-specific direct and half fits stay
  separate. Synthetic true-quality coverage remains unvalidated.
- The production campaign does not reuse five pilot fits: its campaign and
  production-case receipts differ. Repeating those five cases adds <1% of720
  cases and preserves straightforward provenance. No other control refits.

No architecture/selection change, new uncertainty tuning, coverage gate or
scorer promotion is authorized by these implementation conventions.
