# Pilots alongside the confirmatory run — 2026-10-09

All exploratory. They ran while the
[confirmatory capacity run](capacity_confirmatory_protocol_2026_10_09.md) was
fitting, which they did not touch: that run uses a worktree pinned at its
frozen commit.

## 1. Is defense sub-additive in bigs? The 2003–2011 era says partly

`scripts/era_additive_analogues.py`.

- **Forecasts.** Each season is forecast by a ridge additive model fitted on
  the previous season only, with players unseen in that season counted as
  average.
- **Roles.** They come from the previous season's box counts and listed
  height.
- **Residual.** Actual minus forecast, per possession, offense's points per
  100. The season-level means drift, so only differences between rows matter.

| Defensive bigs (6'10"+) | 2003–2011 residual | 2016–2026 residual |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0 | +1.96 ± 0.61 | +2.14 ± 0.43 |
| 1 | +0.41 ± 0.49 | +0.95 ± 0.34 |
| 2 | −0.23 ± 0.45 | +0.63 ± 0.37 |
| 3 | −0.10 ± 0.70 (92,691 possessions) | +0.65 ± 2.07 (26,039) |

Relative to additivity, the first big is worth about 1.5 points of extra
defense and the second about 0.6. The third adds about what additivity
predicts (−0.1 ± 0.7). In 2003–2011, spread offenses beat the additive
forecast by more against bigger defenses: +1.1 against zero or one big, +1.6
against two and +1.75 against three. The SEs are 0.7–0.9, so this is
suggestive only. Four or more bigs never reach 500 possessions in any era.

**Reading.** The diminishing-returns direction of the capacity law is
supported, mostly at the low end. Nothing measures four or five bigs.

## 2. Perimeter coverage shortfall is bounded by real lineups

The shortfall is the number of offensive shooters minus the number of
non-big defenders, floored at zero. It uses the reference's saved forecasts
on all 2016–2026 rows, half blend, with season SEs:

| Shortfall | Possessions | Residual, half blend | Residual, NN calibrated |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0 | 2,052,022 | −0.75 ± 0.13 | −0.51 ± 0.16 |
| 1 | 601,045 | −0.75 ± 0.21 | −0.33 ± 0.24 |
| 2 | 102,000 | −0.79 ± 0.48 | −0.30 ± 0.48 |
| 3+ | 5,479 | −0.04 ± 2.70 | +0.39 ± 2.67 |

The reference misses no shortfall effect that real lineups can show. At
shortfall 2 a missed effect would have to be under about a point. An asserted
coverage cost large enough to sink five centers on its own would contradict
these analogues.

## 3. A best-passer and best-shooter teammate head is inert

`scripts/creation_screen.py`.

- **Fit.** Warm start from the version-2 capacity screen fits, seed 17, eight
  origins, four epochs, against an identically continued control.
- **Head.** `lineups.model.Creation` gives each attacker a correction from
  his own state plus his best-passing and best-shooting teammates' states,
  rather than the teammate mean.

| Creation minus control | Mean ± origin SE |
| --- | ---: |
| r², half, prior calibration | +0.0001 ± 0.0004 |
| Five-Gobert offense vs neutral defense | +0.23 ± 0.13 |
| Legal centers vs Jokić/Westbrook margin | −0.15 ± 0.09 |
| Residual on real cells whose best passer is below 5 assists/100 | −0.02 ± 0.01 |

Properties at 2026q4 are unchanged. The head learns nothing that the
teammate-mean context had not already learned.

## 4. Scoring without selected possessions keeps the ranking

`scripts/late_possession_benchmark.py` scores each cell on its possessions
after the first 25 (4,705 cells with at least 50 such possessions, 678k
possessions). The same possession join reproduces the primary scorecard
exactly.

| Model | Primary r² | Late-possession r² | Late bias |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| J_POINT_BOTH half | .1630 | .1890 | +0.35 |
| NN calibrated | .1615 | .1877 | +0.34 |
| additive_defense | .1460 | .1700 | +0.35 |

The ranking and the neural model's edge (+0.017 primary, +0.019 late) are
unchanged. Forecasts run 0.35 points high on unselected possessions.

## 6. Two-phase training from scratch (in progress at writing)

earlier single-stage check.

- **Phase A** trains the offense alone (`EventModel.offense_only`).

## What the pilots say together

- **Learned terms leave the extremes alone.** Three structures fitted on real
  lineups (coverage assignment, tethered help and specific-teammate creation)
  were all non-inferior and all inert at the extremes.
- **Only asserted pooling moves the extremes.** That is the capacity law of
  the confirmatory run.
- **The era data confirm its direction but cannot size it beyond three bigs.**
- **Five-center perimeter and creation costs remain assumptions.** The
  assumption band is the honest place for them.
