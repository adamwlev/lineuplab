# V20 transport-uncertainty rehearsal protocol

Frozen before reading the new rehearsal outputs,2026-10-06. This implements
§7 of the unchanged V20 plan, not a new NN architecture or arm selection.

Build two empirical descriptor snapshots at the two registered pilot outer
cutoffs,2016q1/2026q4. Historical game×off5 role observations use only observed
FGA/FTA channels (counts and exposure masked together), skip zero team totals,
730-day half-life,1,825-day lookback,200possession league pseudomass and.05SDfloor.
Clock uses31states including unknown, with six margin bins collapsed. Retain
individual player support and which slot/channel caused stretch. No label from
the queried quarter is allowed into these histories. Zero mismatch for a new
player remains explicitly thin/unknown, not evidence of confidence.

Compare descriptors of the original versus compressed saved forecasts where
available. Snapshot construction is shared by all mean models. Native averaged
actor counts are averaged over seeds before forming role shares. The half/additive
blend is not assigned a fictional actor ledger. Real pilot interval fitting must
remain cold/unidentified, with no borrowing of other models' prior residuals.

Transport likelihood: one common bias, persistent variance exp(a)+nonnegative
slopes on (R/RMS_R)² and optionally(C/RMS_C)². Non-centered prior-only scales,
1e-6floor. The existing sum-NLL plus lambda/2 times squared slopes is preserved;
lambda.1/1/10, last completed calibration quarter, strongest1e-10tie, cold10.
Recompute historical noise shrinkage and validation noise pools using only the
subfit's prefix; never use validation-quarter bootstrap noise to predict its
joint error distribution. Slopes may be exactly zero. Numerical acceptance uses
the bound-projected mean gradient<1e-5, not unconstrained gradients at a boundary.

Additional identified-fit synthetic rehearsal (distinct from the already-frozen
profile recovery experiment):20replicates, seeds20200601–20200620. Each generates
eight chronological training quarters of150pairs, spanning two seasons, followed
by300held-out pairs. R uniform[0,3], C uniform[0,.8]. Common bias.5;
persistent variance4+3R²+12C²; independent finite noise200/effective_games,
effective_games uniform[4,15] for each half. Supplied completed-quarter measured
noise is the true finite variance (an intentionally favorable noise-estimation
case). A second constant-variance case uses tau²=9. The two cases reuse the
replicate seed/common random numbers. The model sees noisy paired errors, not
the latent draw or true variance. No extra search over generators, penalties,
seeds or coverage thresholds in response to results.

Fit U1/U_ROLE/U_ROLE_CLOCK on the same training history; score held-out joint
NLL, finite80/95coverage, variance recovery error and learned slopes. Save every
replicate and mean/Monte-Carlo SE. This verifies an identified fitting path and
shows boundary/null behavior; synthetic success cannot validate NBA hypothetical
lineup quality. Missing identification in real pilots is expected, not failure.

Exact temporal, shape, moment, gradient or receipt failures block use. Weak
synthetic recovery, unnecessary slopes or poor coverage are reported, not tuned
away. NN means, original artifacts and full-launch authority remain unchanged.
